# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Egyptian shoppers browsing hwasi.com on mobile, cash-on-delivery buyers looking for fashion basics (pants, polos, basics). They land on a product page from a Meta ad, scan price / fit / delivery trust signals in seconds, and decide to order — or not. The admin/operator (Hussein) runs promotions and merchandising from the backend.

## Product Purpose

An Arabic-first fashion e-commerce storefront that converts ad traffic into delivered orders. Success = orders placed AND delivered (low cancellation rate), driven by trust signals (delivery, COD, size confidence) and a premium-but-simple presentation. The backend scrubs competitor products (Vendoor) into polished listings.

## Brand Personality

Elegant and chic (أنيق وشيك): quiet-luxury fashion feel carried by deep emerald green (#1B4D3E) + gold (#D4AF37), Cairo font, airy white space. Simple and beautiful (بسيطه وجميله) — calm, confident, never loud. Voice: refined Arabic, no slang, no shouting.

## Anti-references

- Aggressive sale styling: red/orange screaming badges, stars, "خصم 90%" clutter, discount-store energy.
- Cluttered/busy promo shapes: too many boxes, tags, and effects fighting for attention.
- Heavy colored borders/frames and thick side-stripes around every card.
- Generic AI cream/sand dashboards or SaaS-gradient looks.

## Design Principles

1. Simple before clever — one clear message per surface; remove before adding.
2. On-brand always — emerald + gold + white; never introduce a foreign accent.
3. Sell through trust — delivery/size/fit confidence beats decoration.
4. Arabic RTL is the default, not a retrofit; typography rhythm serves Arabic.
5. Show, don't shout — premium comes from spacing and restraint, not badges.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (gold on white only at large/bold sizes or as border, never body text), focus states visible, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for any promo motion, RTL layout correctness, tap targets ≥ 44px on mobile.
